## Team Structures

### Closed Structure ^170899

- The person at the top of the hierarchy is not concerned with the things managed by his child managers.

![[../../attachments/Project Management_Drawing 2023-01-31_1.svg|Project Management_Drawing 2023-01-31_1.svg]]

### Random Structure ^5d41e5

- Everyone is talking to everyone but not him/her self.

![[../../attachments/Project Management_Drawing 2023-01-31_2.svg|Project Management_Drawing 2023-01-31_2.svg]]

### Hybrid / Open Structures

- The mixture of [[Project Management#^170899|Closed]] and [[Project Management#^5d41e5|Random]] team structures.

	- Risk identification
	- Risk Analyzing and Assessment
		- Probability

		- Risk Exposure -> Probability x Impact
	- Risk Prioritization
		- Sorting out the priorities
	- Risk Mitigation
		- The process of coming up with strategies to deal with risks.
